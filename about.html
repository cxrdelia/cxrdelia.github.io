ABOUT

I’m currently a third-year (first professional) student at the University of Canterbury, studying a Conjoint Bachelor of Mechatronics Engineering with Honours and International Business. People often ask me why I chose to study my degree, and my answer is that I love the variety and breadth in the subjects I get to study. I enjoy applying both my technical and business knowledge in re al life scenarios, often participating in case competitions and other opportunities through UC.

Outside of my university studies, I am also a keen musician, participating in a variety of local and national orchestras and working freelance as a collaborative pianist and music teacher. I took on the role of music director for the first time in 2025, with the musical Head over Heels by UC’s MUSOC. Conducting & directing is a new skill I am developing, and I am always eager to take on opportunities to further my musical development.
